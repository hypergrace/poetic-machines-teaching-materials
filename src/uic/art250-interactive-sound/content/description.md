In this class, we will learn the basis of electronic and computer music in the visual programming environment Max/MSP. Students will develop a working understanding of the principles of sound synthesis, sample recording and playback, and gestural interfaces to construct programs for their own live electronic performances and interactive installations. The course will engage electronic music genres of drone, ambient, IDM, and techno to anchor principles of sound design, rhythmic sequencing, and composition. Students will earn grades through the creation of two projects: a performance or generative art midterm and a final project, which may be a performance, generative sound system, sound sculpture, or an interactive installation.
